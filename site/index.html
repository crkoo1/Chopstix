The grip alone, no policy: palm held still, finger targets fixed. First gravity at nine hand orientations, then disturbances stepped up until the grip lets go. Reading the numbers

Same episodes every time. Each setup reruns the same seeds (start-joint noise, cube position, place target) with the policy's mean action, so a change between snapshots comes from the policy or the code, not from luck in the draw.

Hold success means both sticks end within 1 cm and 10° of where the grip puts them (palm frame). An episode ends early when a stick moves 3 cm or turns 30°.

Pick success needs all of: the cube pinched to 40 mm, carried to B, lowered, released within 2 cm of B, and both sticks still in the hand. "Ended early" means a stick slipped 3 cm in the hand or the cube was knocked 12 cm from the tips.

Small samples. 32 episodes give intervals about ±15 points around the middle of the scale. Trust a change when the whiskers stop overlapping, or rerun with more episodes.

Training evals use their own fixed seeds and the training settings, so they can differ from the numbers here.
